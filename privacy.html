🔒 Privacy Policy

MahaExam Education Platform • Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to MahaExam. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of personal information entrusted to us by our students.

1. Information Collected

  • Account Details: Basic profile credentials (phone number or name) to identify the student and maintain mock test series progress.

Contextual advertisements are served via standard Google Mobile Ads in full compliance with Google Developer Program Policies.

MahaExam is an independent educational app designed solely for exam preparation assistance. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or an official entity of any government recruiting agency.
